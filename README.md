# 🏠 ToLet Coimbatore

> A hyperlocal room and house rental listing platform built for students and working professionals in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

---

## 🚀 What is this?

**ToLet Coimbatore** is a full-stack web application that connects landlords with tenants — without brokers, without spam, without confusion.

Landlords post real listings. Tenants find rooms with full details, photos, map location, and direct contact. Simple as that.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Finding a room in Coimbatore is painful:

- OLX and NoBroker are too generic — they don't go hyperlocal
- Most listings are outdated or fake
- No map-based search for a specific area
- Brokers add unnecessary cost and friction

**ToLet Coimbatore** solves this with a focused, local-first platform.

---

## 👥 Target Users

| Role         | Who                                                                |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 🎓 Tenants   | Students and working professionals looking for rooms in Coimbatore |
| 🏡 Landlords | House owners wanting to list vacant rooms/houses for free          |

---

## ✨ Core Features

### For Tenants

- 🔍 Search rooms by area, rent range, room type
- 🗺️ Map view showing available rooms nearby
- 📋 Full listing — photos, amenities, rent, deposit, contact
- 🔧 Filter by furnished/unfurnished, AC/non-AC, gender preference

### For Landlords

- 📝 Post a listing with photos and full details
- ✅ Mark room as available or taken
- 📞 Receive direct tenant inquiries — no middlemen

## 🌟 Why This Will Work

- **Real problem** — Coimbatore has a massive student and working population with genuine housing demand
